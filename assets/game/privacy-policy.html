Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Garden of Words is a word puzzle game provided by Görkem Ali CÖMERT. This policy explains what information the app stores or shares when you use the game.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game progress, best times, settings, language preferences, theme choices, and hint balances locally on your device. This information is used to provide the game and is not sent to us unless a feature below requires it.

Leaderboards

If you use the leaderboard, the app may process your chosen nickname, avatar, completion times, and basic technical information such as app language and platform. This information is used to display leaderboard results and player profiles.

Ads and purchases

When ads are enabled, the app may use Google Mobile Ads to show advertising. Premium subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We do not receive your payment card details.

Third-party services

The app may use Firebase for authentication and leaderboard data, Google Mobile Ads for advertising, and RevenueCat for subscription status. These services process information under their own privacy policies.

Data requests

For questions or requests about your data, contact us at gkmcng@icloud.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The current version will remain available from the Help section.
